# 35 · Kubernetes RBAC Abuse

Review a Kubernetes audit record for an unexpected grant of cluster-wide administrative access.

**Track** Intermediate · 12 min · Kubernetes

**Prerequisites** Be comfortable reading timestamps and kubernetes audit projection fields.

## Investigate

**Attacker intent** Elevate cluster permissions.

**Learning outcome** Check the request actor, role reference, subject and API response. Review later activity by the new subject.

1. Review the clean baseline. Kubernetes audit projection supplies the exercise evidence.
2. Consider the hypothesis. An interactive actor granting cluster-admin through a ClusterRoleBinding needs immediate authorization review.
3. Replay the positive fixture and inspect THX-35. Check the request actor, role reference, subject and API response. Review later activity by the new subject.
4. Compare the noisy fixture. An approved bootstrap or break-glass action can grant high privileges temporarily.

## Rule and evidence

Count at least 1 observation(s) per `host` inside 300 seconds. Each distinct source record can count once.

| Field | Comparison | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `action` | `eq` | `"create_binding"` |
| `outcome` | `eq` | `"success"` |
| `observables.resource` | `eq` | `"clusterrolebindings"` |
| `observables.role_ref` | `eq` | `"cluster-admin"` |
| `observables.actor_class` | `eq` | `"interactive"` |

Review the following event-level exclusions before changing the rule.

- `observables.change_context` eq `"approved_bootstrap"`

Tuning guidance. Allow vetted controller identities and track expiration of temporary bindings. Audit edits as a separate behavior.

## Contain and prevent

Preserve audit events and revoke the confirmed unauthorized binding. Inventory later access by the subject.

Use least-privilege RBAC, change review for privileged bindings, and admission controls where appropriate.

## Knowledge check

- Why is this binding high impact?
  - ClusterRoleBinding and cluster-admin grant broad privileges
  - It sorts first in the list

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

ClusterRoleBinding and cluster-admin grant broad privileges. Both binding scope and referenced role matter for effective access.

</details>

- Who may use the new permissions?
  - Only the actor who submitted the request
  - The subject named in the binding

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

The subject named in the binding. The actor performs the grant. The subject receives access.

</details>

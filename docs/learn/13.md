# 13 · Kerberoasting

Correlate service-ticket requests for distinct services alongside encryption and requester context.

**Track** Intermediate · 12 min · AD

**Prerequisites** Be comfortable reading timestamps and windows security or sysmon projection fields.

## Investigate

1. Review the clean baseline. Windows security or Sysmon projection supplies the exercise evidence.
2. Consider the hypothesis. Three distinct legacy-encrypted service tickets requested by one user during ten minutes warrant investigation.
3. Replay the positive fixture and inspect THX-13. Check event 4769, encryption type, requesting identity and the distinct service names.
4. Compare the noisy fixture. Legacy services and trusted inventory jobs can produce bursts of service-ticket requests.

## Rule and evidence

Count at least 3 observation(s) per `user` inside 600 seconds. Count distinct `observables.service_name` values.

| Field | Comparison | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `event_id` | `eq` | `"4769"` |
| `action` | `eq` | `"service_ticket_request"` |
| `observables.ticket_encryption` | `eq` | `"rc4"` |
| `observables.requester_class` | `eq` | `"user"` |

Tuning guidance. Baseline service accounts, isolate legacy protocols and verify ownership before excluding a workload.

## Contain and prevent

Review requester activity and service-account exposure. Move accounts to managed credentials according to policy.

Prefer gMSAs and modern encryption. Audit service principal assignments and legacy-client dependencies.

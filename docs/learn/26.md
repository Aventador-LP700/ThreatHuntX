# 26 · AiTM phishing

Investigate risky session drift after a sign-in and evaluate evidence for a possible relay.

**Track** Advanced · 15 min · Identity

**Prerequisites** Know the basics of Identity auditing and how to review authorization context.

## Investigate

1. Review the clean baseline. Entra sign-in or audit projection supplies the exercise evidence.
2. Consider the hypothesis. An abrupt shift to an unmanaged context on the same session warrants checking sign-in and MFA evidence.
3. Replay the positive fixture and inspect THX-26. Pivot by session and compare network, device and authentication evidence around the initial sign-in.
4. Compare the noisy fixture. A VPN or mobile connection handover can change addresses rapidly.

## Rule and evidence

Count at least 1 observation(s) per `user` inside 300 seconds. Each distinct source record can count once.

| Field | Comparison | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `action` | `eq` | `"session_transition"` |
| `observables.session_delta` | `eq` | `"new_ip_unmanaged"` |
| `observables.time_class` | `eq` | `"rapid"` |

Tuning guidance. Combine device attestation, session continuity and managed network knowledge. Review high-volume VPN exceptions.

## Contain and prevent

Verify the user, revoke the suspicious session and review subsequent application access.

Require origin-bound authentication such as FIDO2 passkeys and apply conditional access to session risk.

## Validation and mapping

Baseline and noisy fixture produce no alerts. The positive fixture produces an alert with linked evidence.

The lab uses summarized session transitions. No sign-in relay or impersonation occurs.

Enterprise ATT&CK 20.1 · related. Session drift supports a relay hypothesis. It does not directly show that an adversary intercepted traffic.

# 20 · Cloud IAM Abuse

Inspect a cloud audit record that grants a privileged role to a principal outside a deployment identity.

**Track** Intermediate · 12 min · Cloud

**Prerequisites** Be comfortable reading timestamps and cloud audit projection fields.

## Investigate

**Attacker intent** Misused cloud permissions.

**Learning outcome** Identify granting actor, recipient, account, policy and the authorized deployment process.

1. Review the clean baseline. Cloud audit projection supplies the exercise evidence.
2. Consider the hypothesis. A successful interactive administrative role grant warrants checking change approval and later role use.
3. Replay the positive fixture and inspect THX-20. Identify granting actor, recipient, account, policy and the authorized deployment process.
4. Compare the noisy fixture. An approved break-glass grant can be necessary during incident response.

## Rule and evidence

Count at least 1 observation(s) per `user` inside 300 seconds. Each distinct source record can count once.

| Field | Comparison | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `action` | `eq` | `"role_grant"` |
| `outcome` | `eq` | `"success"` |
| `observables.role_class` | `eq` | `"administrative"` |
| `observables.actor_class` | `eq` | `"interactive"` |

Review the following event-level exclusions before changing the rule.

- `observables.change_context` eq `"approved_breakglass"`

Tuning guidance. Give emergency exceptions an expiry and independent approval. Retain grants even when alerting is suppressed.

## Contain and prevent

Preserve cloud audit evidence, revoke an unauthorized grant and review resource access by the recipient.

Enforce least privilege, short-lived roles and change approval for administrative grants.

## Knowledge check

- Whose actions should be scoped after a grant?
  - Both the granting actor and target principal
  - Only the dashboard viewer

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Both the granting actor and target principal. The grant records change accountability and potential downstream impact.

</details>

- What qualifies a break-glass exception?
  - Any administrator display name
  - Independent approval, defined scope and expiry

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Independent approval, defined scope and expiry. Emergency exceptions need accountable scope and a deadline.

</details>

## Validation and mapping

Baseline and noisy fixture produce no alerts. The positive fixture produces an alert with linked evidence.

This is a normalized cloud audit projection. Provider actions and effective permissions require separate verification.

Enterprise ATT&CK 20.1 · direct. The behavior in this lesson corresponds to this technique; the fixture models a defensive observation.

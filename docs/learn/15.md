# 15 · Privilege Escalation

Investigate an unexpected transition to root privilege alongside process provenance.

**Track** Advanced · 15 min · Linux

**Prerequisites** Know the basics of Linux auditing and how to review authorization context.

## Investigate

**Attacker intent** Abuse local flaws/rights.

**Learning outcome** Confirm the effective UID, original actor and parent provenance. Compare audit, package and change records.

1. Review the clean baseline. Linux audit projection supplies the exercise evidence.
2. Consider the hypothesis. A regular actor gaining effective UID 0 under an unknown parent warrants review of the authorization path.
3. Replay the positive fixture and inspect THX-15. Confirm the effective UID, original actor and parent provenance. Compare audit, package and change records.
4. Compare the noisy fixture. A controlled privilege broker may intentionally mediate administrative access.

## Contain and prevent

Preserve audit and process lineage, scope follow-on actions and isolate if unauthorized activity is confirmed.

Patch OS and packages, constrain privilege brokers and review privileged execution policies.

## Knowledge check

- Which field reflects privileges after the transition?
  - Effective UID
  - The file extension

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Effective UID. Effective UID is used for privilege checks. The original actor gives accountability.

</details>

- What helps distinguish a valid transition?
  - The host name suffix
  - A verified privilege broker and authorization record

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

A verified privilege broker and authorization record. Confirm how privileges were delegated before concluding exploitation.

</details>

## Validation and mapping

Baseline and noisy fixture produce no alerts. The positive fixture produces an alert with linked evidence.

The privilege transition by itself is a weak proxy for exploitation and is supplied as an inert audit record.

Enterprise ATT&CK 20.1 · related. Unexpected elevation motivates a T1068 investigation. A privilege transition can have other causes.

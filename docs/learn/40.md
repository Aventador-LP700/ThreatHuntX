# 40 · SaaS Exfiltration

Review a cloud application record for a large external share of restricted documents.

**Track** Intermediate · 12 min · SaaS

**Prerequisites** Be comfortable reading timestamps and saas audit projection fields.

## Investigate

**Attacker intent** Mass download/sharing.

**Learning outcome** Check actor, volume, data classification and destination. Review subsequent access and whether sharing was approved.

1. Review the clean baseline. SaaS audit projection supplies the exercise evidence.
2. Consider the hypothesis. A large share of restricted SaaS data to external recipients warrants checking access grants and delivery.
3. Replay the positive fixture and inspect THX-40. Check actor, volume, data classification and destination. Review subsequent access and whether sharing was approved.
4. Compare the noisy fixture. An approved collaboration or e-discovery export can generate a bulk share event.

## Rule and evidence

Count at least 1 observation(s) per `user` inside 300 seconds. Each distinct source record can count once.

| Field | Comparison | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `action` | `eq` | `"bulk_share_summary"` |
| `observables.destination_class` | `eq` | `"external"` |
| `observables.data_class` | `eq` | `"restricted"` |
| `observables.file_count` | `gte` | `100` |

Review the following event-level exclusions before changing the rule.

- `observables.change_context` eq `"approved_discovery"`

Tuning guidance. Scope approvals by destination tenant, actor and data policy. Distinguish access granted from access used.

## Contain and prevent

Preserve share and access logs, revoke unauthorized links and scope external access or downloads.

Apply DLP, external sharing policy, data classification and conditional access.

## Knowledge check

- What establishes that recipients used the share?
  - Access or delivery audit records after the grant
  - The size of a sharing icon

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Access or delivery audit records after the grant. An authorization event can precede access or never be used.

</details>

- What helps prioritize the alert?
  - The first letter of the app name
  - Data class, destination, actor and volume

<details>
<summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

Data class, destination, actor and volume. Sensitivity and destination provide context beyond record count.

</details>

## Validation and mapping

Baseline and noisy fixture produce no alerts. The positive fixture produces an alert with linked evidence.

The dataset records a synthetic share summary. External access or data transfer needs separate evidence.

Enterprise ATT&CK 20.1 · related. External sharing can provide a route for exfiltration over a web service. The fixture represents sharing and not confirmed download.

# Code of conduct

ThreatHuntX welcomes learners and contributors of every background and experience level. Engage with curiosity, respect privacy, and make technical disagreements specific and constructive. Accept corrections, help beginners find reproducible evidence, and credit others’ work and ideas.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted contact, impersonation and disclosure of someone else’s private information are incompatible with participation. Keep discussions and examples within the project’s defensive scope. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and activity that represents the project.

Report concerns privately to the [maintainer](https://github.com/Aventador-LP700). Include how you prefer to be contacted and evidence you are comfortable sharing. The maintainer can remove content, limit participation or ban contributors as circumstances require. Reports will be handled with discretion and reviewed in the context of the incident.
